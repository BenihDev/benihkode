## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how OneThing ("we", "us", or "our") handles your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). We designed OneThing so that almost nothing about you ever leaves your iPhone — this page explains the few exceptions and who they involve.

## The Short Version

OneThing has no accounts, no sign-in and no server. Your OneThings, your timer sessions, your history and your streak live in local storage on your device. We do not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal information, because we never receive any.

## Information the App Keeps on Your Device

Everything the App needs to work is stored locally on your iPhone:

- **Your OneThings** — the task titles you set, their targets, and whether each Day was finished
- **Timer sessions** — start and pause times that make up your Focus
- **Your preferences** — reset hour, reminder hour, reminders on/off, and the share name you optionally set
- **Your purchase** — whether you own *Remove ads*

None of this is transmitted to us. Deleting the App deletes all of it.

## Third-Party Services

OneThing has no server and no analytics of its own. The only network traffic the App generates goes directly to the two services below — Google, when an ad is requested, and Apple, for purchases — never to us, and nothing about you passes through any other party.

- **Google AdMob** — the free version shows banner ads served by Google (AdMob) on the Settings and History screens only, never inside Today. Google may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve and measure ads, subject to your consent choices: where required by law, the App shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested, and you can revisit that choice anytime via **Settings → Privacy options**. Google's own privacy policy explains what it collects: [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Apple payments (StoreKit)** — the *Remove ads* purchase is processed entirely by Apple. We never see your name, payment method or any payment detail; the App only learns from Apple whether the purchase is owned, and keeps that fact on your device. Apple's privacy policy covers the transaction: [apple.com/legal/privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

If you remove ads, ad requests stop and the ad-related data flows above end with them.

## Notifications

Reminders and the target alert are scheduled locally by the App on your device and delivered by Apple's notification system. They are not sent by a server, and their content never includes information that did not already come from you. The **Reminders** switch in Settings stops all of them.

## Live Activities

The running timer can appear on your Lock Screen and in Dynamic Island. This is a system feature rendered on your device by iOS from data that never left it.

## Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 — or from anyone, of any age. The App has no input path through which personal information could reach us.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new Privacy Policy on this page and update the "Last updated" date shown above.
