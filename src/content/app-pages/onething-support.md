## Reporting a problem

If OneThing misbehaves, email us and tell us what happened. To help us reproduce it quickly, please include:

- Your iPhone model and iOS version
- What you were doing when it happened (starting a session, pausing from the Lock Screen, editing a target…)
- What you expected to happen, and what happened instead
- A screenshot, if you can capture one

## Frequently asked

**Where is my data stored?**

On your iPhone, in the app's own local store. OneThing has no account and no server — nothing you set, time or complete is sent anywhere.

**I missed a day. Did I lose my streak?**

One missed day does not erase your history — the missed OneThing carries to today with its past intact. Your streak counts the days you actually finished.

**My day ends too early.**

The Day ends at your reset hour, not at midnight. Open Settings → Day and move the reset hour to when your day actually ends (it defaults to 4 AM).

**How do I stop the notifications?**

Settings → Reminders has a master switch. Off silences the day's reminders and the target alert at once; the timer itself keeps running.

**How do I remove the ads?**

Settings → Ad-free → Remove ads. It is a one-time purchase — no subscription, and it applies to every device signed into the same Apple ID.
